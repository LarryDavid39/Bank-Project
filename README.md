# Bank-Project
An interactive bank dashboard in PowerBI to analyze financial performance, lending activities, customer demographics, and employee trends.
## Key Insights
Total Income stands at approximately $116M, with a 3.4% decline in the selected period.

Annual Income remained relatively stable between $115M - $117M from 2018-2025, showing a fairly consistent revenue base.

UPI and online banking generates the highest income among the channels, at approximately $19.6M each.

POS recorded the lowest income among the six channels at approximately $18.7M

## Customer & Card Performance

The bank has approximately 95K accounts and 19K transactions in the selected view.

Classics card account for the largest share of card-type income at approximately 45%.

The customer age analysis shows Millennials generating the highest income, followed closely by Baby Boomers and Gen X.

## Lending Performance

Total loans issued in the selected period are approximately $48.7M

Home Loans represent the largest loan category at about $14.1M

Gold Loans are the smallest category at approximately $5.5M

Male customers account for 52.23% of loan value among the age group, at approximately $19.2M, while Gen Z accounted foe about $3.7M.

## Workforce Insights

Employee numbers peaked around 2023 at 80 in the yearly employee trend before declining to 30 in 2025.

The dashboard also identifies the longest-serving employee with 26 years of service and the newest employee with 1 year.

# Recommendations

1. Investigate the recent income decline to understand whether it is being driven by transaction volume, customer activity, product mix, or specific branches 
2. Strengthen digital banking channels. UPI and online banking are already strong performers, so further investment in digital products could increase customer engagement and transaction revenue.
3. Focus on the home-loan segment, which currently represents the largest loan category, while identifying opportunities to grow lower-performing loan products.
4. Develop targeted Gen Z products. The relatively low loan contribution from Gen Z suggests an opportunity to introduce products designed around younger customers' financial needs
5. Monitor employee trends closely. The decline in the yearly employee trend from 2023 to 2025 should be investigated alongside recruitment, attrition, and branch staffing requirements.
6. Use branch-level performance monitoring to identify high-performing branches and understand what operational practices are driving results.
